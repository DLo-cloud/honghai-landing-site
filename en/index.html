06Before we begin

A few things
worth asking.

Can I start without a complete specification?

Yes. Share the location, intended use, known conditions and the problem you want to solve. We can clarify the brief together.

Is Cohesol a sourcing partner, manufacturer or installer?

Our role is a solution-led sourcing service partner: we help clarify needs, compare product and supply options, and coordinate supplier communication. Manufacturing, detailed engineering, installation, certification and local compliance are handled by the relevant parties, with responsibilities clarified for each project.

How are scope, fees and product quotations agreed?

We first understand the brief, then agree the sourcing scope and working arrangement. Service fees and product quotations are confirmed for the specific project; product pricing also depends on specifications, quantities, availability and delivery terms.

What kinds of projects can you help with?

Our three main fields are hospitality and kitchen equipment, energy applications, and modular aluminium structures. Share your brief so we can agree how to help.
